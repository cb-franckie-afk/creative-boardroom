What keeps founders awake

04 questions

  1. Collections

    Which collection actually made money last season?

    Not the one that sold the most. The one that was left with margin after markdowns, returns and wholesale terms.

  2. Inventory

    How much cash is trapped in inventory?

    Every unit on the rack is money you already spent. The question is when it comes back, and at what price.

  3. Growth

    At what revenue does growth become dangerous?

    A bigger order book needs bigger production runs, paid up front. Somewhere, growth starts costing more cash than it brings.

  4. Pricing

    Which product pays your rent?

    Most brands live off a handful of styles. Few founders know exactly which ones.

Are you growing? Or just getting bigger?

Revenue is the easiest number to grow and the least useful one to steer by. We look at the numbers underneath it.

Our approach

Not just numbers. The bigger picture.

About

We kept hearing the same problem. For thirteen years.

NOT.A.BOARD is Franck and Jelle. This is why we started it.

  1. Thirteen years ago

    Brilliant at their craft.

    We started talking to fashion entrepreneurs who were exceptional at what they did. Fantastic creatives. But none of them were finance experts, and that is exactly where things kept going wrong.

  2. On the runway

    Backstage, the same story.

    Franck walked three runways as a model, including Amsterdam Fashion Week. Between shows he brought the subject up again with the designers. The problem was still there.

  1. Today

    Ready to sit at the table.

    Since then we built the knowledge that was missing. Franck as a process analyst and now a certified accountant, Jelle as a finance expert and CFO. Today we feel experienced enough to help these founders with confidence.
